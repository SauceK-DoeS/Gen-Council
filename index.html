002 // COGNITIVE ARCHITECTURE

Why Single-Model AI Fails. And Why GEN COUNCIL Wins.

When you prompt a single AI model—whether ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok—you inherit its systemic blind spots, ideological bias, sycophancy, and hallucinations. No single AI possesses monopoly over truth.

⚠️ The Flaw of Single-AI Reliance

  • Hallucination Blindness: A lone AI cannot fact-check its own hallucinations because it believes its own probability weights.
  • Sycophantic Agreeableness: Models frequently tell users what they want to hear rather than pointing out fatal architectural flaws.
  • Monopolistic Lock-In: Your workflow is held hostage to one lab's rate limits, outages, and proprietary filters.

🛡️ The GEN COUNCIL Solution

  • Adversarial Peer Review: Models blindly grade each other on a 30-point rubric with zero knowledge of who wrote what.
  • Flaws ruthlessly exposed: When ChatGPT misses an edge case, Claude penalizes it; when Grok oversimplifies, Gemini critiques it.
  • Hybrid Synthesis: The winner's structure is enriched with the runner-up's unique insights to produce the ultimate answer.

003 // CONSENSUS ARCHITECTURE

Protocol Specification & Scoring Mechanics

A mathematically rigorous, peer-reviewed evaluation framework designed to eliminate hallucination, bias, and sycophancy.

⚖️ The Standardized 30-Point Scoring Rubric

Every AI juror evaluates each peer solution across three orthogonal dimensions, awarding 0 to 10 points per criterion.

DIMENSION 01 // 10 PTS

Prompt Adherence

Did the model directly address every explicit requirement, constraint, and edge-case mentioned by the user? Deductions occur for dodging questions or hallucinating unauthorized assumptions.

DIMENSION 02 // 10 PTS

Logical Rigor & Factuality

Is the factual information accurate and verifiable? Is the internal chain of logic sound, free of self-contradiction, and structurally coherent?

DIMENSION 03 // 10 PTS

Depth & Completeness

Does the response provide non-trivial insights, realistic architectural tradeoffs, and complete working solutions rather than superficial generalizations?

📐 Mathematical Consensus & Scoring Formula

How GEN COUNCIL deterministically calculates scores, rankings, and crowns the official winner.

// DEMOCRATIC PEER SCORE FORMULA:
TotalScore(Mi) = Σj ≠ i ( Adherencej→i + Logicj→i + Depthj→i )
Where j ∈ {ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok} and j ≠ i (Strict Self-Grading Prohibition).
Maximum Theoretical Score = 30 pts × 3 peers = 90 Points.
⚖️ Tie-Breaking Priority

In the event of an exact score tie between two models, Prompt Adherence points act as the primary tiebreaker. If still tied, Logical Rigor points decide the victor.

✨ Top-2 Hybrid Synthesis Principle

The 1st place winner provides the primary structural architecture; the 2nd place runner-up provides unique complementary insights to create a master unified response.

Browser Extension

Ideal for students, writers, and professionals who already use ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok in their web browser.

  1. Open chrome://extensions or edge://extensions
  2. Toggle on "Developer mode" in top-right
  3. Click "Load unpacked" & select the extension folder

PATH 02 // DIRECT CONNECTOR

Model Context Protocol

Directly connect Claude.ai Web or ChatGPT using the open Model Context Protocol (MCP) Streamable HTTP standard.

  1. Run start_mcp_council.bat
  2. Live MCP URL is auto-copied to clipboard
  3. In Claude.ai, click Settings ➔ Connectors
  4. Add custom connector & paste the HTTPS URL
  5. Claude now calls Council tools natively!
